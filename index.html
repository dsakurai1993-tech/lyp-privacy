Privacy Policy – Necklace Notes Publisher

This application is an internal tool operated by LY Partners Inc. (Japan) to manage its own Pinterest business account. It does not collect, store or share personal data of any other Pinterest users.

It accesses only the data of the operator's own account (boards, Pins and Pin analytics) through the Pinterest API, and uses it solely to publish content and review its performance. Access tokens are stored on the operator's computer and are not shared with third parties. We do not store Pinterest login credentials or session cookies.
